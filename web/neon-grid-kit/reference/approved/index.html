DESIGN NOTES

A view over calls.
One source of truth.

Each channel shows the latest 500 characters of transcript, stopping sooner at its adaptive look-back boundary. Older words leave this view; the original calls remain in history.

01

Read the channel
Short, timestamped transmissions. Newest at the bottom. Stable channel order, so updates don’t move your place.

02

Open any call
See its full words first, followed by the surrounding exchange. Every line leads back to its source call.

03

Stay honest about freshness
Pending text, quiet channels, and failed transcription are distinct. Nearby calls are context, not proof of a shared incident.

This standalone concept uses fictional, in-memory calls and sample window widths. “Add demo call” advances the sample clock by 18 seconds. No audio or live service is connected.
